Fitbit Calendar Sync

Sleep, naps and workouts in a private Google Calendar.

Fitbit Calendar Sync is a personal Google Apps Script. It reads sleep sessions and workouts from the Google Health API (read-only) and adds them as private, "free" events to a calendar named Fitbit in the same Google account. It runs every few hours inside that account. There is no server, and no data leaves Google.
